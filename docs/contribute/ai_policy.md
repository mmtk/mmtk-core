# AI Policy

In this policy, a *contribution* is any content that an account submits to MMTk projects, such as
code, documentation, pull requests, issues, and comments. This policy applies to contributions that
include content generated by AI tools, such as large language models (LLMs).

* **AI-generated content is allowed.** You may use AI tools to help produce your contributions.
* **A human must be behind the account.** You must submit contributions and engage with the
  community from your own account, e.g. responding to reviews and discussions. We currently do not
  allow fully automated accounts (e.g. accounts operated by AI agents) to make contributions.
* **You are responsible for your contributions.** You are responsible for all the content of your
  contributions, regardless of how it was produced.
  * **Self-review.** We strongly recommend that you understand your contributions, verify their
    facts and review the code before making them public and requesting review from others
    ([draft pull requests](../team/code_review.md#draft-pull-requests) are exceptions as they are
    normally not reviewed).
  * **Disclosure is optional.** You may indicate AI use (e.g. a `Co-authored-by` or `Assisted-by`
    commit trailer, or a note in the pull request) to help reviewers.
* **Licensing.** You must ensure that your contributions can be distributed under MMTk's license
  (see [COPYRIGHT](../../COPYRIGHT)), and that they do not include content from sources with
  incompatible licenses.
* **We review the content, not the tool.** Contributions are reviewed based on their content and
  quality, not on whether AI was used. However, low-quality or careless contributions may result in
  lower priority when reviewing future contributions from the same account.
